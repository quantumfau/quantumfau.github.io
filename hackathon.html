How teams work

Built for the mix, not by luck

Every team has up to four people: one from the College of Business, one from the College of Engineering & Computer Science, a Science student where we can, and one open seat for any major. Within those rules we match on shared interests, overlapping availability, and complementary roles, so you land with people you’ll actually work well with. Your team is announced before the event and set up on DeepStation for you.
