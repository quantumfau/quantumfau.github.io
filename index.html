What We Do

A club built like a startup studio

Small interdisciplinary teams pick a real quantum or AI problem, build a working prototype, and package it for an audience beyond the classroom.

Build

Students from every background — engineering to design to policy — form project pods and ship working prototypes: quantum circuits, ML models, and the case for why they matter.

Learn

Workshops and speaker sessions cover quantum computing fundamentals, applied AI, and the strategy, ethics, and communication side of deep-tech ventures.

Launch

Finished projects become conference submissions, research collaborations, portfolio pieces, and warm introductions to internship partners.
