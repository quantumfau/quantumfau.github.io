Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 — Florida State Championship

FAU is the home base for the first-ever Qiskit Fall Fest Florida State Championship — a statewide quantum-computing competition run by Entangled Solutions Group with FAU. It runs in three stages: workshops and registration (Sept 14–30), local university hackathons (Oct 2–30), and the state championship at FAU’s Boca Raton campus on Nov 13, 2026. Teams from six Florida universities — FAU, Miami-Dade College, Nova Southeastern, FGCU, Embry-Riddle, and Florida Tech — compete, with beginner-friendly workshops to get you started. No experience needed to learn.

Quantum Beach 2026

Florida’s flagship quantum conference — one day at the Palm Beach County Convention Center in West Palm Beach on Oct 22, 2026 (9:30 AM–4 PM, plus a cocktail hour). It brings together researchers, founders, students, and investors around quantum computing, post-quantum security, and the growing quantum economy. No quantum background required.
