Find your project pod.

Fill this out once. We'll place you in a small project pod — four to six people, matched by what you want to work on and how you like to work, with the mix of majors every project needs.

Your response goes straight to the club organizers. No quantum background required — just curiosity.

How it works

From a room of strangers to real teams

Every pod is four to six people, with at least two from the College of Business — because turning research into something real takes strategy and storytelling as much as code. The rest of each pod is matched on shared interests, overlapping schedules, and complementary strengths: a builder, a researcher, a strategist, a communicator.
