Programs

Three ways in, one project cycle.

Every member joins a track that matches their strengths — technical, strategy, or creative — then gets placed on a cross-track project pod. Here's how the tracks, and the semester, actually work.

Technical Track

Build the thing

For CS, engineering, physics, math, data science majors — or anyone curious enough to learn quantum or ML tooling from scratch.

  • ✓ Qiskit / Cirq / PennyLane quantum programming
  • ✓ Applied machine learning & data pipelines
  • ✓ Weekly build sessions with code review
  • ✓ Paper reading group on current research
Business & Strategy Track

Make it fundable

For business, finance, economics, entrepreneurship majors — anyone who can turn a prototype into a case worth backing.

  • ✓ Market & competitive research
  • ✓ Pitch decks, grants & case-competition prep
  • ✓ Sponsor, faculty & industry partnerships
  • ✓ Product & go-to-market strategy
Creative & Communications Track

Make it land

For design, communications, journalism, marketing, or humanities majors — anyone who can make a hard idea click for an audience.

  • ✓ Visual design for decks, demos & posters
  • ✓ Science communication & explainer writing
  • ✓ Social, newsletter & event promotion
  • ✓ Photo/video documentation of pod work
The Project Cycle

One semester, one shipped project

Pods form early and work the whole semester toward a single concrete deliverable.

01 — Weeks 1–2

Form & Frame

New members choose a track, get placed on a 3–5 person pod, and scope a problem at the intersection of quantum, AI, and a real-world use case.

02 — Weeks 3–10

Build & Iterate

Technical members build the circuit, model, or pipeline; strategy members run research and shape the case; creative members design and write how it's presented — all syncing weekly.

03 — Weeks 11–15

Ship & Submit

Pods present at the end-of-semester showcase and package the project for a conference submission, application, or portfolio piece.

Weekly Rhythm

What a typical week looks like

General Meeting

Whole-club session — guest speakers, workshops, or project showcases. Open to all.

Track Workshop

Skill-building split by track: quantum/AI tooling for Technical, strategy & pitch craft for Business, design & storytelling for Creative.

Pod Work Session

Project teams meet independently to build, research, and check progress against the cycle timeline.
