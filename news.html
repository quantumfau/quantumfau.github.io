What's happening in quantum at FAU

Florida Atlantic is building one of the most ambitious university quantum programs in the country. Here's what's new — and why it's a good time to get involved.

Just added · College of Business

FAU launches a quantum computing course for business

FAU's College of Business has introduced a new Executive Education certificate, Quantum Computing: Business and Sourcing Strategy — bringing quantum out of the lab and into managerial and procurement training for working professionals. It's a clear signal of what this club is about: quantum isn't just for engineers and physicists, it's for the business and strategy side too.

More from FAU quantum

The bigger picture

A snapshot of the research, hardware, and hires putting FAU on the quantum map.

Hardware

Florida's first onsite quantum computer

FAU signed a $20 million agreement for a D-Wave Advantage2 system, making it the first university in Florida to host public, onsite quantum hardware right here in Boca Raton.

Read more

Leadership

A director for the Center for Quantum Technologies

Robert Loredo was named executive director of FAU's Center for Quantum Technologies, taking the helm of the university's quantum strategy, research, and workforce education.

Read more

Research

Quantum machine learning to predict heart disease

An FAU engineering team led by Arslan Munir built a quantum machine-learning framework that reached over 90% accuracy predicting heart disease — a real-world glimpse of what quantum-assisted AI can do.

Read more

Cryptography

NIST picks an FAU-co-developed encryption standard

NIST selected HQC — an algorithm co-developed by FAU's Edoardo Persichetti — as a new post-quantum encryption standard, part of the work to keep data secure against future quantum computers.

Read more

Engineering

The Quantum Computing Initiative

The College of Engineering's Quantum Computing Initiative anchors FAU's program, partnering with IBM Quantum on research across quantum computing, security, and communications.

Read more

Across campus

A quantum ecosystem spanning every college

Beyond engineering, FAU's Schmidt College of Science lists faculty across seven departments — from physics and math to biology and urban planning — working on quantum. It's genuinely interdisciplinary, which is exactly the point of this club.
