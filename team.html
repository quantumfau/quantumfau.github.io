Team

Run by students, for students.

The club is organized by an officer team spanning both technical and business colleges. Swap in real names and photos as officers are elected.

P

President

Officer position

Sets club direction, runs general meetings, and represents the club to FAU faculty and Student Government.

VT

VP of Technical Projects

Officer position

Leads the Technical Track — workshop curriculum, project pod formation, and code review standards.

VB

VP of Business & Partnerships

Officer position

Leads the Business Track and owns relationships with sponsors, faculty, and internship partners.

T

Treasurer

Officer position

Manages club budget, funding requests, and reimbursement for conference travel and materials.

E

Events & Logistics Chair

Officer position

Plans workshops, speaker sessions, and the end-of-semester project showcase.

M

Marketing & Outreach Chair

Officer position

Runs the club's socials, recruitment campaigns, and this website.

Interested in an officer role?

Officer applications open each spring for the following academic year — reach out any time to get involved early.
