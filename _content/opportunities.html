How Projects Become Opportunities

From working prototype to real outcome

Step 1

Ship a working project

Pods finish the semester with a demoable result: a notebook, a model, a circuit, a validated business case, or a portfolio piece.

Step 2

Package it for an audience

The Business and Creative tracks help shape the project into an abstract, pitch deck, or application-ready portfolio piece.

Step 3

Submit, apply, or present

Members submit to conferences, apply to internships with the project in hand, or pursue faculty-mentored research credit.
