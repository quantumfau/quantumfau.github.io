Hackathon · Hosted at FAU

Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 — Florida State Championship

Sept–Nov 2026 · FAU Boca Raton campus (championship)

FAU is the home base for the first-ever Qiskit Fall Fest Florida State Championship — a statewide quantum-computing competition run by Entangled Solutions Group in partnership with Florida Atlantic University. Qiskit is IBM’s open-source quantum programming toolkit, and the Fall Fest is a season of learning and building around it.

It runs in three stages:

  • 1.Workshops & registration (Sept 14–30) — beginner-friendly sessions, in person and virtual, to get you up to speed.
  • 2.Local university hackathons (Oct 2–30) — each campus runs its own event; winners advance.
  • 3.State championship (Nov 13, 2026) — local winners compete for the Florida title at FAU Boca Raton.

Six Florida universities take part — FAU, Miami-Dade College, Nova Southeastern, Florida Gulf Coast, Embry-Riddle, and Florida Tech. You register through FAU during the September window; sponsors award prizes to the winners. No prior quantum experience needed to jump in and learn.

Conference · West Palm Beach

Quantum Beach 2026

Thu, Oct 22, 2026 · Palm Beach County Convention Center, West Palm Beach

Quantum Beach is Florida’s flagship quantum-technology conference — a single day bringing researchers, founders, students, and investors together to move quantum from the lab toward real-world use. It’s organized by the Florida Alliance for Quantum Technology (FloridaQuantum.org).

The program runs 9:30 AM–4:00 PM (doors at 9:00), followed by a 4–5 PM cocktail hour, and covers quantum computing, post-quantum cryptography, and the wider quantum ecosystem. As the organizers put it: no quantum background required. For QDC members it’s an easy trip — the convention center is about 10 minutes from FAU’s Boca campus, an 8-minute walk from the Brightline station, and 10 minutes from Palm Beach International.
