About Us

Two majors. One build.

The Quantum Development Club exists to close the gap between people who can build quantum and AI systems and people who can turn that work into something the world funds, publishes, or hires for.

Our Mission

Connect technical and business students to build things that leave the classroom.

Quantum computing and applied AI are moving fast enough that campus clubs can do genuinely original work — but only if the team has both the engineering to build it and the strategy to place it somewhere that matters. We recruit across FAU's colleges on purpose, pair students deliberately, and measure success by what leaves the club: a paper, a demo, a conference talk, an internship offer.

In one sentence

We turn interdisciplinary student teams into shipped quantum & AI projects — and shipped projects into real opportunities.

Who It's For

You don't need a quantum background to join

Every team is built around complementary skill sets, not identical resumes.

Engineering & CS students

Comfortable in Python and ready to learn quantum SDKs, ML frameworks, or both — no prior quantum coursework required.

Physics & math students

Bring the theory — quantum mechanics, linear algebra, information theory — and help teams reason about what's actually possible.

Business & finance students

Own the market research, pitch narrative, partnerships, and case-competition strategy that gets a technical project noticed.

What We Value

The principles behind how we run projects

Ship, don't simulate

Every pod aims for a working artifact — a notebook, a deck, a demo — not just a discussion.

Pair on purpose

Technical and business students are placed together deliberately, not left to self-sort by major.

Open door, real bar

No major or GPA requirement to join — but projects are held to a standard worth showing off campus.

Opportunity is the output

Every project cycle is designed to end in something concrete: a submission, an application, an introduction.
