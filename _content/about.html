About Us

Every major. One build.

The Quantum Development Club exists to close the gap between the people who can build quantum and AI systems, the people who can make them matter, and the people who can explain them to everyone else.

Our Mission

Connect students across every discipline to build things that leave the classroom.

Quantum computing and applied AI are moving fast enough that a student club can do genuinely original work — but only with people who can build it, people who can place it somewhere that matters, and people who can make it land with an audience. We're recruiting across FAU's colleges from the start, on purpose.

In one sentence

We're building a home for students from every major who want to work on quantum computing and AI — together.

Who It's For

You don't need a quantum background to join

We're looking for complementary skill sets, not identical resumes.

Engineering & CS students

Comfortable in Python and ready to learn quantum SDKs, ML frameworks, or both — no prior quantum coursework required.

Physics, math & science students

Bring the theory — quantum mechanics, linear algebra, biology, chemistry — and help teams reason about what's actually possible.

Business, finance & policy students

Own the research, pitch, and strategy that gets a technical idea noticed and funded.

Design & communications students

Own the visuals, writing, and story that make a technical idea click for an audience beyond the club.
