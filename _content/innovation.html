What makes a pod

Small teams, built to actually ship.

Every pod is four to six people, and no pod is all engineers or all business majors. At least two members come from the College of Business, because turning research into something real takes strategy and storytelling as much as code. The rest of each pod is filled to line up shared research interests, overlapping schedules, and complementary strengths — a builder, a researcher, a strategist, a communicator.

Every pod, guaranteed
  • ✦4–6 students per pod
  • ✦2 business students in every pod
  • ✦Matched on interests, schedule & working style
  • ✦A mix of majors and degree levels on purpose
