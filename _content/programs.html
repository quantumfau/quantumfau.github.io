Programs

Two tracks, one project cycle.

Every member joins a track that matches their strengths, then gets placed on a cross-track project pod. Here's how the tracks — and the semester — actually work.

Technical Track

Build the thing

For students writing the code, running the circuits, and training the models.

  • ✓ Quantum programming with Qiskit, Cirq, or PennyLane
  • ✓ Applied machine learning & quantum-ML hybrid methods
  • ✓ Weekly build sessions with code review
  • ✓ Paper reading group on current quantum/AI research
  • ✓ Hardware access via cloud quantum simulators & providers
Business Track

Make it matter

For students who turn a working prototype into funding, coverage, or a hire.

  • ✓ Market & competitive research for deep-tech applications
  • ✓ Pitch deck development & investor-style Q&A prep
  • ✓ Grant, case-competition, and conference-abstract writing
  • ✓ Sponsor, faculty, and industry partnership outreach
  • ✓ Product & go-to-market strategy for technical projects
The Project Cycle

One semester, one shipped project

Pods form early and work the whole semester toward a single concrete deliverable.

01 — Weeks 1–2

Form & Frame

New members choose a track, get placed on a 3–5 person pod, and scope a problem at the intersection of quantum, AI, and a real-world use case.

02 — Weeks 3–10

Build & Iterate

Technical members build the circuit, model, or pipeline; business members run research and shape the narrative in parallel, syncing weekly.

03 — Weeks 11–15

Ship & Submit

Pods present at the end-of-semester showcase and package the project for a conference submission, application, or portfolio piece.

Weekly Rhythm

What a typical week looks like

General Meeting

Whole-club session — guest speakers, workshops, or project showcases. Open to all.

Track Workshop

Skill-building split by track: quantum/AI tooling for Technical, strategy & pitch craft for Business.

Pod Work Session

Project teams meet independently to build, research, and check progress against the cycle timeline.
